Four quiet surfaces. That's the whole app.

Your year, on one screen

The Timeline is home: every month of the year in one calm scroll. Months you've written glow with life, months to come stay faint, and the one you're in wears a soft pearl sheen. You always know where the year stands.

One honest entry a month

No daily prompts, no guilt. When a month ends, sit down for ten minutes and write what actually happened: a title, a few moments, a photo or two. Plain markdown, a natural editor. Twelve entries a year, each one worth keeping.

Goals that live on the calendar

A yearly goal you never look at is just a wish. In Getsu you drag each goal onto the month where it will happen, so the year has a shape and every month has a job. Check goals off as they land and watch progress add up.

Daily is a chore. A year is a blur.

A month is just right: often enough to remember, rare enough to stay calm. You show up about once a month, and that's the whole point.

Progress, not streaks

Goals add up across the year instead of living or dying by a fragile chain. Miss a month and nothing breaks.

Yours, on your disk

Runs fully offline. Open a folder you own and your journal is written as plain files. Export any time. Sync only if you want it.

Yours to keep.

Getsu is open source, so you can run it yourself. Your journal is plain files you can export any time, and we never sell your data. The core journal is free; Premium only adds cross-device sync, more photos, and cloud backup.
